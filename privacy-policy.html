Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

HotMenu helps restaurants share their menus and manage table orders. This policy explains what information is involved when you visit our website, contact us, or use the HotMenu service.

1. Who this policy covers

HotMenu provides software to restaurants and their guests. A restaurant decides how it operates its menu and service. Depending on the applicable privacy law and the context, the restaurant may be responsible for information it collects through its use of HotMenu, while HotMenu processes that information to provide and protect the service. HotMenu is responsible for information it collects directly, such as a restaurant contact's demo enquiry.

2. Information we handle

Restaurant and staff information

To set up and operate a restaurant account, the service may handle the restaurant or organization name and contact details, staff names and email addresses, role and permission details, and account and security records. Passwords are handled as credentials and are not intended to be stored in readable form.

Menu, table and order information

The service processes restaurant-authored menu content, table and QR identifiers, table-session and order details, item selections, quantities, prices, order status and related operational records. This information is needed to display the menu, route orders to restaurant staff and support the restaurant's service and records.

Diner sessions

Diners can browse and order without creating an account. HotMenu is designed not to ask diners for a name, phone number or email address. A random session identifier stored in an essential browser cookie is used to keep a diner connected to the correct table session. A restaurant may choose to enable an optional display name; if provided, it is associated with that table session and is not a diner account.

Enquiries and technical information

If you email us, we receive the email address and any other information you choose to include. Our website also loads typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser may make a request to Google to retrieve them. When the service is used, hosting, content-delivery and security systems may process technical request information, such as an IP address, to deliver the service and help prevent abuse.

3. How we use information

  • To provide menus, table sessions, ordering and restaurant operations.
  • To create and administer restaurant and staff accounts, and respond to support or demo enquiries.
  • To protect accounts and the service, prevent misuse, troubleshoot issues and maintain operational records.
  • To meet applicable legal obligations and resolve disputes.

If location-based promotions are enabled, their audience may be configured using geographic areas such as a pincode, district or state. This is based on the promotion and restaurant settings, not on building a personal diner profile.

4. Cookies

The ordering service uses essential cookies for session and authentication functions, including keeping a diner session associated with a table. Blocking these cookies may prevent parts of the service from working. The HotMenu marketing website does not require an account or ordering session.

5. When information is shared

We share information as needed with the restaurant using the service and with service providers that support hosting, storage, email delivery, content delivery or security. Those providers process information to provide their services. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, restaurants, HotMenu or the service. We do not sell diner personal information.

6. Retention and security

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain restaurant and operational records, meet legal obligations and handle disputes. Retention can vary by the type of information and the restaurant's use of the service; contact us or the restaurant for a request relating to a particular record.

HotMenu uses safeguards intended to protect accounts and keep restaurant data separated. No online service can promise absolute security, and users should protect their account credentials and contact us if they believe an account has been compromised.

7. Your choices and requests

For information held by a restaurant in connection with a meal or order, please contact that restaurant first. For information HotMenu holds about your account or a message you sent us, email hello@hotmenu.in. We will review requests and respond in accordance with applicable law. We may need to verify your identity or retain some information where the law or legitimate operational needs require it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service or applicable requirements change. The effective date above indicates when this version took effect. Material updates will be made available on this page.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@hotmenu.in or by post to HotMenu, Kochi, Kerala, India.
